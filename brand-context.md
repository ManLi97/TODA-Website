## Origin Story

TODA is named after its founders: **To**mek + **Da**na.

Tomek watched Dana — his partner and a tattoo artist — come home
frustrated after work. Not because of the tattooing itself, but because
of everything around it. No-shows without deposits. Hours of back-and-forth
messages that went nowhere. Two clients showing up at the same time because
the calendar had failed.

Tomek spent five hours reading forums and comments from the tattoo
community. He found the same problems everywhere. There was no real
solution.

Dana called him on her way home — after a day with two no-shows, no
money earned — and asked if he could at least build her a simple online
calendar. Tomek said: "Come home, I have something better."

Two months later, the first version was ready. Sandra joined shortly
after, bringing her own experience as a tattoo artist and her studio
Manas Ink. Manuel joined as the second developer. TODA grew from a
personal problem into a platform built by people who know the craft
from the inside.

## The Real Problems — In the Community's Own Words

These are not invented pain points. They come directly from real
conversations in the tattoo community:

- *"Was kostet Tattoo?"* — no context, no details, just the question
- *"Was letzte Preis, machst du billiger für Freund?"*
- Hours of back-and-forth messages, then: *"Ist mir zu teuer, tschüss."*
- A client who stops responding after two weeks because the process
  was too exhausting
- Two clients standing at the door at the same time
- Booking requests spread across Instagram DMs, WhatsApp, and Facebook
  — all in separate chats, all with different information
- "Where did that reference image go? Which chat was it in?"
- Coming home after a full day of tattooing and having to work through
  40 unread messages before the evening can begin

**The structural issue:** Most tattoo artists are solo self-employed.
They are simultaneously artist, manager, accountant, and customer
service. There is no shop manager, no assistant, no structure — just
a phone with too many apps and not enough hours.

## What TODA Actually Does

1. **Structured first request** — The client fills out a guided form:
   body placement, photo of the placement, motif description, reference
   images, style, contact details, date of birth. The artist receives
   everything at once and can give a price estimate immediately.

2. **Booking page included** — No separate website needed. Gallery,
   about section, categories — all configurable without a web developer.

3. **Self-booking calendar** — The artist sets working hours, the client
   picks a free slot. Appointments stack seamlessly. Double-bookings are
   structurally impossible.

4. **One link for everything** — Whether on Instagram, WhatsApp, or
   Facebook: always the same link, always the same structured intake
   form. Platform-independent.

5. **Consultation appointments** — Optional, configurable as free or
   paid.

6. **PDF export per appointment** — For accounting and personal records.

**Coming soon (publicly communicated):** Integrated deposit handling is
the top priority on the roadmap.

## Tone of Voice

**What TODA sounds like:**
- Direct, no detours
- Understands the craft from the inside — not from a distance
- Speaks to tattoo artists as peers, not as users
- Confident without being arrogant
- Dry humor is allowed — but never forced or performative

**What TODA does not sound like:**
- "Revolutionary AI-powered solution for the tattoo industry"
- Generic SaaS promises
- Startup pitch language
- Hyped, salesy, superlative-heavy

**Phrases that feel right — drawn from real conversations:**
- "Wir sind Künstler, keine Buchhalter."
- "Mehr Kunst, weniger Chaos." — emerged organically, not from a
  brainstorming session
- "Von Tätowierern für Tätowierer."
- "Damit du dich auf das Wesentliche konzentrieren kannst." — the
  essential thing is tattooing, not administration
